Call

Make a room for each group you talk to, like family or friends. Send its invite link once, and everyone can join it any time.

    Rooms are kept per browser: the home screen app and Safari don't share them. To move them, copy them all here and paste them there with Paste invite links.

    Calls go straight between devices and are encrypted end to end. Nothing is stored on any server.
