Call check

A one-minute test that shows how video calls can work on your phone and network. When you're done, copy the results and send them to whoever sent you this link.

Right now you are on

1. Network

Asks a few public address helpers (Google, Cloudflare) which internet address and port your network gives you, the same thing every video call does. Takes about five seconds. Try it once on mobile data and once on wifi if you can.

2. Camera and microphone

Starts your camera and sends it through a call connection inside this page. Nothing leaves your phone.

More checks for iPhone

3. Floating video

Floats the call video in a small window, like FaceTime.

4. Another app

Close the floating video first. Tap Start, switch to another app for about 20 seconds while talking, then come back here.

5. Switching while floating

The floating window switches every 3 seconds between you and a coloured card, as if someone else started talking. Tap, go to another app, watch it for about 15 seconds, then come back.
