iPhone check

Checks what this phone allows a web call to do: float the video, and keep going in another app. Nothing leaves this phone.

1. Camera and microphone

2. Floating video

Tries to float a video that comes through a call connection, exactly like a person in a call.

3. Another app

Close the floating video first. Tap Start, switch to another app for about 20 seconds while talking, then come back here.

4. Switching while floating

Like the app: a hidden video floats and switches every 3 seconds between you and a coloured card, as if a different person started talking. Tap a button, go to another app, watch the floating window for about 15 seconds, then come back.
